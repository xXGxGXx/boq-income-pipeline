Fees and commission

Defaults follow Gumroad's published 2026 rates as reported by several fee guides: 10% + US$0.50 per direct sale, card processing on top at about 2.9% + US$0.30, and a flat 30% on Discover sales that already covers processing. The fixed fees are charged in US dollars, so they're shown here converted at about A$1.50 per US$1; change them to match today's rate. Rates change, so check Gumroad's fees page before relying on these numbers. Gumroad collects and remits GST as merchant of record, so this planner doesn't deduct it from your share.
