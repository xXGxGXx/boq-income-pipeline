## Title
NDIS SIL Compliance Starter Pack — Module 5A Workflow, Word Templates and Evidence Tracker

## Subtitle
A starting point for Australian SIL providers organising Core Module and Module 5A evidence. Not legal advice.

## Description

From 1 July 2026, Supported Independent Living (SIL) must be delivered under registration group 0138. Registration rules changed in October 2026: unregistered providers had until 1 October 2026 to apply. This pack helps you organise a first draft of evidence. It does not register you, pass an audit, or replace guidance from the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission.

### What's inside (version 2.0)
- **Registration and audit-readiness workflow** (Word and Markdown). It covers:
  - the four registration pathways;
  - what a certification audit against the Core Module and Module 5A assesses;
  - an honest self-assessment;
  - evidence of daily practice;
  - a 30-60-90 day plan, audit-day preparation, and an ongoing compliance calendar.
- **11 editable Word templates** (also as plain Markdown):
  - Module 5A self-assessment workbook;
  - support-worker induction and competency record;
  - SIL service agreement outline (supports kept separate from tenancy);
  - supported decision-making record;
  - safeguarding risk register and safeguard map;
  - incident register;
  - complaints and feedback register;
  - continuous improvement register;
  - tenancy and support separation checklist;
  - certification audit-day checklist;
  - document register.
- **Evidence tracker spreadsheet** (Excel) with every Module 5A standard and Core area, Have / Partial / Need drop-downs, and a summary that updates itself.
- **11 AI prompts** for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot. They draft policies and procedures shaped to your organisation, and they mark anything you must check as [VERIFY] instead of guessing. Includes a policy gap reviewer and audit interview practice.

### How to use
1. Read the workflow and confirm your registration pathway
2. Run the self-assessment. Paste in the official Module 5A quality indicators, then rate yourself honestly
3. Use the prompts and templates to close the gaps
4. Keep evidence of daily practice, not only pre-audit paperwork, and track it in the spreadsheet

### What's NOT included

This pack does not file a registration, complete a certification audit, or guarantee a Commission outcome. Prompt outputs are drafts you must review and edit. Official requirements can change.

### Disclaimer

This is an educational starter resource, not legal advice, and not a substitute for professional or NDIS Commission guidance. Always verify against current NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission requirements. Registration rules changed in October 2026. No registration, audit, or compliance outcome is guaranteed.
