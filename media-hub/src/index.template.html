Library location

Privacy & network

Your library is walled off: it is never uploaded, indexed or searchable online, and the page’s own security policy blocks every connection except this device and the Google Drive items you choose to open.

Shared space

The only place meant for other people, kept apart from your private library. Nothing gets in unless you share it. Everyone gets one folder, named after them. It travels only when you send a share pack or sync a folder you chose.

Voice & commands

Tap the mic and say what you want: “show me unseen videos from drive”, “open generated”, “file this in sourced”, “next”. Hold the mic (or say “keep listening”) for hands-free. The same words work typed in the search box or Quick actions.

Folders & sources

Link a folder to read it in place (nothing copied), share phone folders through the companion server, or write the whole library out as a real Media Hub Library folder with AI, Sources, Photos, Videos and your own folders inside.

Privacy

The PIN and screen cover keep casual eyes out. They do not encrypt the files or the catalog stored in this app.

Encrypted notes

Seal private notes with a passphrase (AES-256, on this device). The passphrase is never stored and cannot be recovered. Notes lock again with panic, the PIN screen, or after 5 minutes away. Encrypted notes stay encrypted in backups and are never searchable.

Restore points

Snapshots of your own layer (favourites, ratings, notes, tags, folders and filing, collections, Trash, saved searches, resume points). One is made each day you use the app and before big changes: restoring a backup, deleting for good, importing a catalog, filing 25+ items, removing note encryption. When you encrypt a note, restore points keep only the sealed version. The last 12 are kept on this device. Media is never part of them and never touched.

Your files

Add photos or videos from this device. Bytes are stored exactly as they are: nothing is converted, resized or recompressed, and the originals stay where they are. Each file gets a SHA-256 so exact copies are spotted.

Insights

Backup

Favourites, ratings, notes, tags, progress and settings in one small JSON file. Media files are not included; re-add them and they re-link by hash.

Live server (optional)

Talks to your phone-local Termux server. Only loopback addresses are accepted, so a token can never be sent anywhere else. Works fully offline when the server is not running.

Not connected.

Companion kit

The loopback server, the playable-copy tool, the Tasker player, the Termux one-tap start, the rebuild scripts and the app source travel inside this file. Download them as a zip when you need them; nothing is fetched from the internet.

Self-test

Checks the catalog, stored files, crypto, storage and offline cache so problems show up here instead of failing silently.
