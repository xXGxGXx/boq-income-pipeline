# NDIS SIL Compliance Starter Pack

**Price target: $37 AUD** (system bundle format)

Mandatory pain-pill for Australian NDIS providers delivering Supported Independent Living (SIL).

From 1 July 2026, SIL must be delivered under registration group **0138 – Assistance with supported independent living**. Unregistered providers had until 1 October 2026 to apply. Those who applied continue while assessed; those who did not must stop delivering SIL. New entrants must be registered before starting.

Providers need evidence for Core Module + new Module 5A (supported decision-making, safeguarding, practice governance, tenancy/housing/support agreements). Certification audits apply.

## What's included
- 8 ready-to-use AI prompts (policies, checklists, self-assessment, induction)
- Editable template outlines (Markdown/Word-ready)
- Step-by-step workflow for registration and audit prep
- Self-assessment checklist mapped to Module 5A
- Induction and worker records starter

## How to use
1. Read the workflow doc
2. Run prompts in Claude/ChatGPT/Grok to generate your customised documents
3. Adapt the templates to your organisation
4. Keep evidence for your audit
