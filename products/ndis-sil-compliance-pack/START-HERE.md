# NDIS SIL Compliance Starter Pack: start here

A practical kit for Australian providers of Supported Independent Living (SIL) who are getting ready for registration and a certification audit under registration group 0138: the Core Module plus Module 5A.

**This pack is a starting point, not legal advice.** It doesn't register you, pass an audit or guarantee any outcome. Always check the current requirements of the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission and the NDIS (Provider Registration and Practice Standards) Rules. They can change.

## What's inside

| Folder | What it is | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| `1-workflow/` | Registration and audit-readiness workflow (Word and Markdown) | Work out your pathway, plan 90 days, prepare for audit day, and keep evidence flowing afterwards |
| `2-prompts/` | 11 AI prompts for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and similar tools | Draft policies and procedures shaped to your organisation, then review and edit them |
| `3-templates/word/` | 11 editable Word templates | Self-assessment, induction and competency, agreements, registers, decision-making records, audit-day checklist |
| `3-templates/markdown/` | The same 11 templates as plain text | Paste them into Notion, Google Docs or any system you already use |
| `3-templates/evidence-tracker.xlsx` | A spreadsheet of evidence for each standard | Track what evidence you have, where it is, who owns it, and when it was last reviewed |

## How to use it (about two weeks part-time for a small provider)
1. **Read `1-workflow/registration-and-audit-workflow`.** Confirm your pathway in Step 1.
2. **Run the self-assessment** (`module-5a-self-assessment-workbook`). Add the official quality indicators for each standard from Schedule 7A, then rate yourself honestly.
3. **Fill the gaps.** Use the prompts to draft or refresh policies, and the templates to start registers and records.
4. **Put it into practice.** Auditors look for evidence that your policies are actually followed: records, training, participant input. Polished documents alone aren't enough.
5. **Track evidence** in `evidence-tracker.xlsx`, and use the audit-day checklist before your audit.

## Using the prompts safely
- Never paste a participant's name, NDIS number, health information or other personal details into an AI tool. Use roles and placeholders instead, for example "Participant A".
- Read every draft in full. AI tools can state rules, timeframes or section numbers that are wrong or out of date. Check each one against the Commission's guidance before you rely on it.
- Make every document yours: your services, your structure, your words. Auditors can tell when a policy isn't followed in practice.

## Official sources to check
