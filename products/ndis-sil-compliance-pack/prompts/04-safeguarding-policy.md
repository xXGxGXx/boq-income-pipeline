You are helping an Australian NDIS provider of Supported Independent Living (SIL), registration group 0138, prepare for registration and a certification audit against the NDIS Practice Standards: the Core Module and Module 5A (supported decision-making; safeguarding; practice governance; agreements about tenancy, housing and support arrangements).

Rules for your answer:
- Use Australian English and plain language a support worker can follow.
- Do not invent legislation section numbers, timeframes, official wording or requirements. Wherever something must be checked against current NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission guidance or the Rules, mark it **[VERIFY]**.
- Make it specific to the organisation details below, not generic.
- This is a draft for the provider to review and adapt; do not present it as legal advice.

Organisation details:
- Name: [organisation]
- Size: [number of homes] homes, [number] participants, [number] workers
- Setting: [metro/regional/remote, state or territory]
- Who oversees quality and safety: [roles, governing body]

Task: write a safeguarding policy for our SIL homes.

Do we use, or might we need to use, regulated restrictive practices? [yes/no]

Include:
1. How risks of abuse, neglect, exploitation and harm are identified, assessed and reviewed, including each home's risk register and safeguard map
2. Prevention: culture, worker screening, supervision, participants' independent connections, and safe ways to raise concerns
3. Responding: immediate safety, preserving evidence, support for the person, and internal reporting lines
4. Reporting outside the organisation: police, the NDIS Commission's reportable incident scheme, guardians and others. Mark every timeframe and category **[VERIFY]**
5. Learning from incidents and complaints
6. Restrictive practices: say plainly that regulated restrictive practices have separate requirements (a behaviour support plan, authorisation in the state or territory, and reporting), and that unauthorised use is a reportable incident. Don't give legal advice; point to specialist advice
7. Training and how competency is checked
For each section, give the policy text and one example of good evidence.
