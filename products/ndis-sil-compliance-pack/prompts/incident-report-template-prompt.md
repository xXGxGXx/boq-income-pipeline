# Prompt: Incident Report Template

Create a practical incident report template for a SIL provider suitable for daily use and audit evidence.

Fields to include:
- Date, time, and location of incident
- Participants and staff involved
- Description of what happened (factual, no opinion)
- Immediate actions taken
- Injuries or harm (if any)
- Notifications made (internal, family/advocate, NDIS Commission, police)
- Follow-up actions and owners
- Sign-off by reporter and supervisor

Also generate a short companion prompt that turns a completed incident report into a continuous-improvement action item.

Organisation: [insert name]

Output the template as a fillable form and the companion prompt as a separate short block.
