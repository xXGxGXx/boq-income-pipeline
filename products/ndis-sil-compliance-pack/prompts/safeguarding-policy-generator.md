# Prompt: Safeguarding Policy Generator

Generate a safeguarding policy for a SIL provider covering Module 5A safeguarding requirements.

Include:
- How risks to participants are identified, assessed, and managed
- Incident prevention, reporting, and response steps
- Mandatory reporting obligations (including to police and the NDIS Commission where required)
- Restrictive practices: when they may be used, authorisation requirements, and documentation
- Participant rights, including how to raise concerns without fear of reprisal
- Staff responsibilities and training on recognising abuse and neglect

For each section provide the policy text plus one example of what good evidence looks like.

Organisation: [insert name]
Services: [insert]
Do you use or may you use restrictive practices: [yes/no]

Output as a policy document. Note: restrictive practices require separate authorisation processes — flag this clearly rather than giving legal advice.
