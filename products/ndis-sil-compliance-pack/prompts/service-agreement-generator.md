# Prompt: SIL Service Agreement Generator

Generate a service agreement outline for a SIL provider that clearly separates housing and support where relevant, per Module 5A.

Include sections for:
- Parties and commencement date
- Support services to be delivered and how they are described
- Housing arrangements (if applicable) kept distinct from support
- Fees, payment terms, and review process
- Participant rights and responsibilities
- Complaint and feedback process
- Incident reporting obligations
- Termination and transition arrangements
- Privacy and information handling

For each section, give the clause text plus a note on what evidence supports it.

Organisation: [insert name]
Support type: [insert]
Housing included: [yes/no]

Output as an agreement outline the provider can adapt. Flag that legal review is recommended before use with participants.
