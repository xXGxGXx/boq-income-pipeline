# Prompt: Supported Decision-Making Policy

You are an NDIS compliance specialist. Draft a supported decision-making policy for a SIL provider aligned with Module 5A and the NDIS Practice Standards.

Cover:
- The presumption of decision-making capacity and how staff uphold it
- When and how to use supported decision-making tools (communication aids, advocates, family involvement)
- How to document decisions the participant made versus decisions made on their behalf
- When to escalate to a formal decision-maker or guardian
- Staff training requirements on supported decision-making

For each section, include a short policy statement, a practical example, and an evidence item an auditor might request.
