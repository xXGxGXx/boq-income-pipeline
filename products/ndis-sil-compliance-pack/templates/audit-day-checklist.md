# Certification audit-day checklist

Organisation: [name] · Auditor: [approved quality auditor] · Audit dates: [stage 1] [stage 2] · Coordinator: [name]

## Two weeks before
- [ ] Audit scope, stages, sites and timetable confirmed in writing with the auditor
- [ ] Self-assessment workbook updated, and every rating has evidence behind it
- [ ] Evidence tracker complete: each item has a location and an owner
- [ ] Document register current: owners, versions, approval and review dates
- [ ] Policies are the approved versions, and workers know where to find them
- [ ] Worker files sample-ready: screening checks, induction, training, competency, supervision
- [ ] Participant files sample-ready: agreements, plans, decision-making records, risk information
- [ ] Registers up to date: incidents, complaints, continuous improvement, risks
- [ ] Participants and supporters told about the audit in an accessible way. Interviews are voluntary, and consent is recorded
- [ ] Interview times suit participants' routines and communication needs. A trusted supporter is available if wanted
- [ ] Key people rostered to be available: managers, a governance representative, workers from different shifts

## Two days before
- [ ] Re-check anything that was open on the gap list
- [ ] Meeting room, access to records, and a contact person for the auditor arranged
- [ ] Workers briefed: be honest, describe what you actually do, and it's fine to say "I'd check with my supervisor"

## On the day
- [ ] Opening meeting attended by: [names]
- [ ] Requests log kept (what the auditor asked for, who provided it, when)
- [ ] Closing meeting notes taken, including any non-conformities and their timeframes

## After the audit
- [ ] Corrective action plan for each non-conformity, with an owner and due dates
- [ ] Actions entered in the continuous improvement register
- [ ] Governing body briefed on [date]
- [ ] Mid-term or next audit date noted: [date]
