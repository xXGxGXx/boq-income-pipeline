# SIL service agreement outline

> This outline helps you structure a service agreement for supports. **Keep tenancy or accommodation arrangements in a separate agreement.** Have the final agreement reviewed against current NDIS requirements, and the tenancy law of your state or territory, before use. Not legal advice.

Provider: [legal name, ABN, NDIS registration number] · Participant: [name] · Nominee or supporter (if any): [name, role]

Agreement start: [date] · Review date: [date] · Plan dates: [from to]

## 1. About this agreement
- What this agreement covers: Supported Independent Living supports. It does not cover housing or tenancy.
- How the participant was supported to understand it: [format used, interpreter or supporter, time given to consider it, questions answered].
- The participant's right to choose and change providers.

## 2. The supports
| Support | Where | When, and how often | Shared or individual | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

- How the participant's goals, will and preferences shape day-to-day support.
- Roster of care or support schedule: attached, and how changes are agreed.
- Supported decision-making: how the participant will be supported to make decisions about their supports.

## 3. Prices and payment
- The prices for supports, and the price arrangements and limits they follow.
- How claims are made, and how the participant or their plan manager can query them.
- What happens to prices when supports are shared with other residents.

## 4. Responsibilities
- The provider's responsibilities: safe and quality supports, communication, privacy, notice of changes.
- The participant's responsibilities, written respectfully and in plain language.

## 5. Changes, notice and ending the agreement
- How either party can change or end the agreement, and the notice periods.
- How support continues during a transition to another provider.
- **What changing support provider means for the participant's home.** If housing is provided by the same organisation or a related one, explain this clearly and fairly.

## 6. Feedback, complaints and incidents
- How to give feedback or make a complaint to the provider, in accessible ways.
- How to complain to the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission, and to an advocate.
- How incidents are managed and reported.

## 7. Privacy and information
- What information is collected, why, who it is shared with, and how the participant can see or correct it.

## 8. Conflicts of interest
- Any relationship between the support provider and the housing provider, and how conflicts are managed.
