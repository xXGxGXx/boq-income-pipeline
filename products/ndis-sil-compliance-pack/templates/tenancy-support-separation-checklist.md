# Tenancy and support separation checklist

Home: [name] · Housing provider: [name] · Support (SIL) provider: [name] · Completed by: [name] · Date: [date]

Use this checklist where the same organisation, or related organisations, provide both the housing and the SIL supports. Check every item against current NDIS guidance and the tenancy law of your state or territory. Not legal advice.

## Agreements
- [ ] The tenancy or accommodation agreement is a separate document from the service agreement
- [ ] Each agreement says what it covers and what it does not
- [ ] Each participant was supported to understand both agreements, with time to consider them and access to a supporter or advocate
- [ ] Copies were given in each participant's preferred format

## Choice and control
- [ ] Participants know they can change their support provider
- [ ] It is written down what happens to each person's housing if they change support provider
- [ ] Changing support provider does not, by itself, end a participant's tenancy (if it would, explain why, and get advice)
- [ ] Participants have a say in who they live with, and in decisions about their home

## Conflicts of interest
- [ ] Any relationship between the housing and support providers is disclosed to participants in plain language
- [ ] A conflict-of-interest policy covers housing and support, and is followed
- [ ] Complaints about housing and complaints about supports can be made separately, and escalated independently

## Money
- [ ] Rent and board are charged and recorded separately from support charges
- [ ] Participants or their nominees can see what they pay for housing, and what they pay for supports
