# NDIS SIL Compliance Workflow

## Step 1: Confirm your pathway
- Already registered (e.g. under 0115)? Commission varies your certificate to add 0138. Comply with Module 5A from 1 July 2026; assessed at next audit.
- Applied before 1 October 2026 as unregistered? Continue delivering while application is assessed.
- Did not apply by 1 October 2026? Stop delivering SIL immediately.
- New provider: Must be registered before starting SIL.

## Step 2: Gather evidence for Core Module + Module 5A
Module 5A covers:
1. Supported decision-making
2. Safeguarding
3. Practice governance
4. Agreements about tenancy, housing and support arrangements

## Step 3: Use the prompts
Open prompts/ folder. Paste into your AI tool with your organisation details. Review and edit every output.

## Step 4: Complete templates
- sil-self-assessment.md
- induction-checklist.md
- service-agreement-outline.md

## Step 5: Maintain ongoing evidence
Auditors look for daily practice, not just pre-audit paper. Keep registers, training records, incident logs, participant agreements.
