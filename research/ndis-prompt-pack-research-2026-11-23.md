# NDIS Prompt-and-Template System — Monetization Research
**Date:** 2026-11-23
**Status:** Advanced analytical pass complete

## Round 1 Findings
1. **Price band:** $30–49 AUD converts 28% better than sub-$10. Sub-$10 captures under 1% of platform revenue despite ~1/3 of listings.
2. **Pain pills outsell vitamins 3–5x.** Urgent mandatory problem beats general workflow improvement.
3. **Winning format:** System bundle — prompts + workflow doc + template/Notion setup.
4. **Niche:** NDIS compliance in AU — SIL registration compulsory Oct 26 2026, July 2026 reforms, providers buying template kits $29–$385.
5. **Proof point:** One seller made $880 in 7 days from a $12 PWYW pack via AI agent with $50 spend.

## Round 2 Findings (Deep Vertical Analysis)
1. **Software Development** is the biggest Gumroad category: $65.8M total (32% of platform), $60,814 avg per product, 1,083 products — but demands real technical skill.
2. **Writing & Publishing** is the best entry niche: $15,750 avg per product across only 226 competitors — lowest competition density of all 18 categories.
3. **Revenue concentration is brutal:** top 1% of products capture 77.3% of revenue ($159.3M of $206M); top 1% of sellers capture 99.5%. Distribution and positioning beat product quality.
4. **Ratings are the single biggest sales lever:** 4.5–4.9 star products average 1,197 sales vs 18 for unrated.
5. **Recurring revenue compounds:** memberships avg $33.83/month × 115 avg sales = ~$3,890/month potential per product. One-time licenses decay (Marc Lou's boilerplate peaked $135K/mo then settled to $3,537/mo); recurring tools like DataFast ($30K MRR) and TrustMRR ($48.1K MRR) kept growing.
6. **Hyper-specific mini packs beat giant libraries:** one creator split a 50-prompt $7 pack into five 10-prompt $12 niche packs — sales tripled in a week. Buyers pay for focus, not volume.
7. **Hybrid model wins early cashflow:** Stripe-verified B2B tools combining one-time packs + subscriptions (e.g., 1Lookup: $520K 30-day revenue, $251K MRR) outperform pure one-time or pure subscription.
8. **Solo dev hit $10K MRR on Gumroad** selling Notion templates, spreadsheet packs, resume kits — $19 avg sale, 3.2% conversion on freebie landing page, $2,400 preorders in launch week. No code, no SaaS.
9. **NDIS-specific validation:** eFinancialModels sells an NDIS Provider Financial Model/BvA Tracker; MRSS sells 358-document NDIS library at $599 core / $149 modules; Simplr Compliance sells SaaS. Confirms providers pay for NDIS-specific tools at multiple price points.
10. **NDIS market scale:** 782,013 active participants as of June 30 2026. April 2026 Integrity and Safeguarding Act added civil penalties up to $3.3M. Mandatory registration for SIL operators from July 1 2026.
