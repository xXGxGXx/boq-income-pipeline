**Core structure (PDF / Markdown pack, ~40–60 prompts + templates):**

1. **Quote & Proposal Generator**
   - Prompt: Detailed quote from job notes (materials, labour, exclusions, GST).
   - Prompt: Professional proposal email to homeowner/client.
   - Sample: “Act as a licensed QLD builder. Draft a clear, professional quote for [job description]. Include itemised materials, labour hours at $[rate], exclusions, validity period, and GST. Use plain English.”

2. **Variation & Scope Change Letters**
   - Prompt: Variation request letter (reason, cost impact, timeline).
   - Prompt: Client approval email for extra work.
   - Sample: “Write a polite but firm variation notice for additional electrical work discovered on site. State original scope, new items, price difference, and request written approval before proceeding.”

3. **SWMS & Safety Admin**
   - Prompt: SWMS draft for common high-risk tasks (working at heights, electrical, excavation) tailored to Australian WHS.
   - Prompt: Toolbox talk summary.
   - Sample: “Generate a concise SWMS for roof work on a residential site in QLD. Cover hazards, controls, PPE, and emergency procedures. Align with AS/NZS and QBCC expectations.”

4. **Payment Chasing & Admin**
   - Prompt: Polite payment reminder (7 days overdue).
   - Prompt: Final notice / debt recovery language (compliant).
   - Prompt: Invoice description from job notes.
   - Sample: “Draft a friendly but clear email chasing an overdue invoice of $[amount] for [job]. Reference invoice number, due date, and offer to discuss if needed.”

5. **Payday Super Compliance Checklists & Prompts**
   - Prompt: Calculate and document qualifying earnings and 12% SG for a pay period.
   - Prompt: Checklist for ensuring super reaches fund within 7 business days.
   - Prompt: Response to ATO enquiry or employee query on super.
   - Sample: “Create a simple payday super checklist for a small trade business paying weekly. Include qualifying earnings definition, timing, STP reporting, and record-keeping steps. Note year-1 risk-based ATO approach.”

6. **Bonus templates**
   - Quote acceptance form snippet.
   - Variation register table.
   - Weekly admin review prompt.

All prompts are written for ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Includes usage notes, disclaimer (not legal/tax advice; user responsible for compliance), and QLD-specific references (QBCC, ATO Payday Super resources).
